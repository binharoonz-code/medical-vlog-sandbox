# Omni Flash hook openings — the 12 other approved videos (draft for AG's approval, 30 Oct 2026)

Same protocol as T6 (section 11 of the handover, omni-clip-pipeline v3.3): one 10 s run per video, three hook scenes of about 3.3 s each, one dramatic acted action per scene, hyper-expressive faces, crash zooms and whip pans, ASMR. The headline is the video's screen hook, in the top third from the first frame.
Run on AG's PC only: Google Flow, **Omni 1.1 Flash · Video · Frames · 9:16 · 720p · 10 s · x1 = 15 credits per run**. Regenerate once at most, then stop and report.
Lane check: sharp words (kill, alcohol, injection or needle, sexual) stay out of the spoken lines and only appear in the headline (Lane 3 SPLIT). AG is never on screen.
If Flow garbles the Arabic headline, keep the clip. The headline gets overlaid in assembly.

Cost: 12 runs × 15 = **180 credits**, and up to 360 if every run were regenerated once. That's about AED 6.6 for 180, an estimate scaled from the handover's 30 credits ≈ AED 1.10.
