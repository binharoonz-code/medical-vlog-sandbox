## #7 Brain scan surprise
- Facts: Vernooij 2007 *NEJM*, 2,000 people, mean age 63, no symptoms: silent infarcts 7.2%, aneurysms 1.8%, benign tumours 1.6% (mostly meningioma). Level B.
- Hook: woman ~60 holding brain scan — "They found something on my brain scan!" · AR "لقوا شي في أشعة المخ!" · Screen: Brain scan surprise?
- EN: Breathe. You're not alone. Researchers scanned two thousand healthy people with no symptoms, average age sixty-three. About one in fourteen had a silent stroke they never knew about. Almost two in a hundred had a small aneurysm. More than one in a hundred had a benign tumor, mostly meningiomas. Almost none caused symptoms. Many of these findings are simply watched over time. But a silent stroke is a warning: control your blood pressure, your sugar, and quit smoking. And a sudden worst headache of your life, new weakness, or slurred speech? That's the ER. Right now.
- AR:
  - تنفّسي… مب بروحج، بصراحة
  - دراسة على ألفين شخص صحتهم زينة، بدون أعراض، متوسط العمر ٦٣
  - واحد من كل ١٤ عنده silent stroke ما درى عنه، حوالي ٢٪ عندهم aneurysm صغير، وأكثر من ١٪ ورم حميد أغلبه meningioma
  - أغلبها بس تتابع… بس الـ silent stroke إنذار: انتبهي للضغط، السكر، والتدخين
  - صداع مفاجئ "أقوى صداع في حياتج"، ضعف، أو ثقل في الكلام = طوارئ فوراً

## #9 Balding at 25
- Facts: ~30% of men by 30, ~50% by 50 (Hamilton, via JAMA Dermatology and reviews; mainly Caucasian data; Gulf data unknown). Minoxidil 5% and finasteride 1 mg (men) = Level A (training data).
- Hook: man mid-20s at mirror — "I'm only twenty-five and going bald!" · AR "عمري ٢٥ وقاعد أصلع!" · Screen: Balding at 25?
- EN: Relax. You're not cursed, you're common. Classic studies estimate about thirty percent of men have pattern hair loss by thirty, and about half by fifty. It's mostly genes, plus a hormone called DHT slowly shrinking your follicles. The key is timing. Treatment works best on hair you still have. Minoxidil and, for men, finasteride are the most proven options. They take three to six months to show results, and finasteride needs a doctor's advice first. So don't wait until it's gone. And if your hair falls out suddenly in round patches, that's a different condition. Get checked.
- AR:
  - هدّ أعصابك… بصراحة، إنت مب بروحك
  - تقديرات كلاسيكية: حوالي ٣٠٪ من الرياييل عندهم صلع وراثي على عمر ٣٠، وتقريباً النص على ٥٠
  - السبب جينات وهرمون DHT يصغّر البصيلات مع الوقت
  - العلاج يشتغل أحسن على الشعر اللي باقي: minoxidil، و finasteride للرياييل بعد استشارة دكتور… ويبالهم ٣ لين ٦ شهور
  - وإذا الشعر طاح فجأة على شكل بقع دايرية… هذا شي ثاني، روح افحص

## #12 Testosterone at 40 (Omni Lane 3 — the word "testosterone" stays out of the Omni clip)
- Facts: Massachusetts Male Aging Study (Feldman 2002, men 39–70): total T fell 1.6%/yr within men (0.8%/yr cross-sectional); bioavailable 2–3%/yr. Earlier "~1%/yr" figure corrected.
- Hook: man ~40 on gym bench — "I'm forty and my body's falling apart!" · AR "عمري أربعين وجسمي قاعد ينهار!" · Screen: Testosterone crash?
- EN: Not so fast. Every gym influencer says testosterone crashes after forty. Here's the data. A big study followed more than a thousand men aged forty to seventy for up to ten years. Total testosterone dropped about one and a half percent a year. Real, but a slope, not a cliff. And lifestyle matters a lot. Belly fat is strongly linked to lower testosterone, and poor sleep pulls it down too. So before you buy boosters or injections, fix your sleep, move more, and lose the belly. Low energy, low libido, other symptoms? Get a proper morning blood test, repeated, with your doctor.
- AR:
  - على هونك! بصراحة، كل واحد في الجيم يقول التستوستيرون "ينهار" بعد الأربعين
  - دراسة تابعت أكثر من ألف ريّال من عمر ٤٠ لين ٧٠، تقريباً ١٠ سنين
  - الـ total testosterone نزل حوالي ١.٥٪ في السنة… نزول تدريجي، مب انهيار
  - الكرشة وقلة النوم ينزلونه أكثر… صلّح نومك، تحرك، ونزّل الكرشة قبل لا تشتري boosters
  - تعب وضعف رغبة وأعراض ثانية؟ سوّ فحص دم الصبح، ويتعاد، مع دكتورك

## #13 You're shrinking
- Facts: ~0.6–1 cm per decade after 40, faster after 60–70, women more; men losing ≥3 cm over 20 y had higher mortality (British Regional Heart Study).
- Hook: grandfather at doorframe — "I'm shorter than last year!" · AR "صرت أقصر من السنة اللي طافت!" · Screen: Shrinking every year?
- EN: You might be, and you're not alone. After forty, most of us start shrinking, roughly half a centimeter to one centimeter every decade, faster after sixty, especially in women after menopause. Part of it is your spinal discs slowly drying out. Part is posture. But part can be silent fractures in your spine from weak bones. In one British study, men who lost three centimeters or more over twenty years had a higher risk of dying. So measure your height every year. Lost three to four centimeters? Ask your doctor about a bone density scan. Strength training and enough protein help protect your bones.

  - يمكن صدق! بصراحة، بعد الأربعين أغلبنا يبدأ يقصر شوي شوي
  - تقريباً نص سنتي لين سنتي كل عشر سنين، ويزيد بعد الستين… خصوصاً الحريم بعد سن اليأس
  - السبب: الديسكات تنشف، الوقفة، وأحياناً كسور صامتة في الظهر من هشاشة العظام
  - دراسة بريطانية: الرياييل اللي قصروا ٣ سم أو أكثر خلال ٢٠ سنة، خطر الوفاة عندهم أعلى

## #14 Sperm counts halved? (Lane 3 — "sperm/fertility" stays out of the Omni clip)
- Facts: Levine 2022 meta-analysis, 223 studies 1973–2018: concentration −51.6%, total count −62.3%. **Disputed** (counting methods, sampling; Danish recruits showed no drop over 15 y; 2022 Nat Rev Urol review).
- Hook: man ~30 reading phone — "Men today are weaker than our grandfathers!" · AR "رياييل اليوم أضعف من أجدادنا!" · Screen: Sperm count halved?
- EN: Is that true? Let's check. A huge analysis of over two hundred studies claims sperm concentration fell by about half between 1973 and 2018. Sounds terrifying. But scientists disagree. Critics say counting methods and who got tested changed over the decades, and one study of Danish military recruits found no drop at all. So the truth is probably somewhere in the middle. What's not controversial is what hurts sperm today: smoking, extra weight, and constant heat, like a laptop on your lap. And the big one in gyms: testosterone or steroid injections can shut sperm production down. Planning a family? Protect your swimmers.

  - يعني الحقيقة يمكن في النص

## #15 37.2 isn't a fever
- Facts: Obermeyer 2017 *BMJ* (35,488 patients): mean 36.6 °C, 95% range 35.7–37.3; Protsiv 2020 *eLife*: men ~0.59 °C cooler than men born early 1800s. Fever ≥38 °C; infant <3 months ≥38 → urgent (NICE, training data).
- Hook: mother with thermometer — "37.2! He has a fever!" · AR "٣٧.٢! ولدي فيه حرارة!" · Screen: 37°C = normal?
- EN: Actually, that's probably normal. We were taught thirty-seven is normal, but that number comes from the eighteen hundreds. A study of more than thirty-five thousand patients found today's average is closer to thirty-six point six, and anything from about thirty-five point seven to thirty-seven point three can be normal. Stanford researchers found men today run more than half a degree cooler than men born in the early eighteen hundreds. So thirty-seven point two isn't automatically a fever. Doctors usually call it fever at thirty-eight. But a baby under three months with thirty-eight or more? Come in straight away.

  - دراسة على أكثر من ٣٥ ألف مريض: المتوسط اليوم حوالي ٣٦.٦، والطبيعي من ٣٥.٧ لين ٣٧.٣
  - باحثين في Stanford لقوا الرياييل اليوم أبرد بأكثر من نص درجة من أيام زمان
  - يعني ٣٧.٢ مب بالضرورة حمى… الحمى عادة من ٣٨

## #16 10,000 steps was marketing
- Facts: origin widely traced to 1965 Japanese pedometer "manpo-kei"; Ding 2025 *Lancet Public Health* (57 studies): 7,000 vs 2,000 steps → 47% lower all-cause mortality (HR 0.53), 38% lower dementia; small gains beyond 7,000; observational, moderate certainty.
- Hook: young man staring at watch — "Only seven thousand steps? Today's wasted!" · AR "بس سبع آلاف خطوة؟ راح اليوم علي!" · Screen: 10,000 steps = marketing?
- EN: Wasted? Not even close. Ten thousand steps didn't come from a lab. It's widely traced to a Japanese pedometer from the 1960s called manpo-kei, literally ten-thousand-step meter. Now the science. A 2025 analysis pooled dozens of studies. Compared with two thousand steps a day, seven thousand was linked to about forty-seven percent lower risk of death and thirty-eight percent lower risk of dementia. Beyond seven thousand, the extra benefits were small for most outcomes. More is still fine, but seven thousand is a powerful target. And in summer heat? Walk the mall, not the street at noon.

  - يرجعونه لعداد خطوات ياباني من الستينات اسمه manpo-kei… يعني "عداد العشر آلاف خطوة"
  - دراسة ٢٠٢٥: ٧ آلاف خطوة مقارنة بألفين… خطر الوفاة أقل ٤٧٪ والخرف أقل ٣٨٪

## #17 8 glasses of water?
- Facts: Valtin 2002 (no evidence for 8×8); Yamada 2022 *Science*, 5,604 people, 23 countries: water turnover ~1 to >10 L/day; hot humid climates higher. Over-drinking → hyponatraemia (training data).
- Hook: woman with 8 glasses — "I haven't drunk my eight glasses yet!" · AR "بعدني ما شربت الثمان أكواب!" · Screen: 8 glasses? Says who?
- EN: Relax. That rule has no science behind it. When a physiologist went looking for the evidence in 2002, he found none. In 2022, researchers measured real water turnover in over five thousand people across twenty-three countries. It ranged from about one liter to more than ten liters a day, depending on body size, activity, and climate. And hot, humid places like ours push your needs way up. Food counts too. So forget counting glasses. Drink when you're thirsty, aim for pale yellow urine, and drink more in the heat. But don't overdo it. Huge amounts, too fast, can dangerously drop your sodium.

  - دراسة ٢٠٢٢ على أكثر من ٥ آلاف شخص في ٢٣ دولة: الاحتياج من لتر لين أكثر من ١٠ لترات

## #18 Stop cleaning your ears
- Facts: AAO-HNS 2017 cerumen guideline — counsel against cotton swabs; no ear candling; drops/irrigation/clinician removal for impaction.
- Hook: man holding cotton buds — "I clean my ears every single day!" · AR "أنظف أذني كل يوم!" · Screen: Stop cleaning your ears
- EN: Please don't. Put the cotton bud down. Earwax isn't dirt. It's your ear's self-cleaning system. It traps dust, helps fight bacteria, and slowly moves itself out as you talk and chew. The American ENT guideline actually tells doctors to warn patients against cotton swabs, because they push wax deeper, scratch the canal, and can even injure the eardrum. Skip ear candles too. Just wipe the outer ear with a towel after a shower. Ear feels blocked, or you wear hearing aids? See a doctor. We can soften the wax with drops or wash it out safely. Sudden hearing loss with pain or bleeding? Come in.

  - لا تسويها! بصراحة، نزّل الـ cotton bud… الشمع مب وساخة
  - الأذن تنظف نفسها: الشمع يمسك الغبار، يحارب البكتيريا، ويطلع بروحه مع الأكل والكلام
  - الإرشادات الأمريكية تقول: لا تستخدم الأعواد… تدفع الشمع لداخل وممكن تجرح الطبلة
